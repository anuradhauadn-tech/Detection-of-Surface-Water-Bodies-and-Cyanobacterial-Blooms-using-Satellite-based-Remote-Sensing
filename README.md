# Detection-of-Surface-Water-Bodies-and-Cyanobacterial-Blooms-using-Satellite-based-Remote-Sensing

This project focuses on environmental monitoring of lakes and reservoirs in Sri Lanka using satellite remote sensing data from Google Earth Engine (GEE). Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery was used to generate high-resolution surface water maps, overcoming cloud-cover limitations common in optical satellite observations. The approach was validated across three study sites located in different climatic regions of Sri Lanka.

In addition, Sentinel-2 time-series imagery was utilized to monitor the spatial distribution of cyanobacterial blooms, which pose significant risks to water quality, ecosystems, and public health. The developed workflow demonstrates the potential of combining SAR and optical satellite data for accurate water resource monitoring and early detection of harmful algal blooms, supporting sustainable environmental management and decision-making.
